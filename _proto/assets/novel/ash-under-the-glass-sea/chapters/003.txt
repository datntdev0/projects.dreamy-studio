Dr. Halvard Roe had been the city's last hydrologist, and he still wore the grey coat of the Water Office although the Office had closed before Ines was born.
"You should not have photographed it," he said, holding her print to the window. "You should not have touched it. And you most certainly should not have come to me."
He poured her a glass of something clear and handed it over. It tasted of stone.
"Ines, what I am about to say will sound like an old man's fable. Under the Glass there is still a sea. The city was built on a lid."
