They went out at night, when the Glass was cool and the patrols stayed in their stations. Roe carried a hand drill older than either of them, and Ines carried the brush.
The object had gone dark again. Roe knelt beside it and put his ear to the ribbing, as a doctor listens to a chest.
"A valve," he said. "A pressure valve. Somebody wanted to be able to let it out slowly."
"Let what out?"
"The weight." He sat back on his heels. "Forty years of heat held down, Ines. If this opens, the city does not fall in. It rises."
