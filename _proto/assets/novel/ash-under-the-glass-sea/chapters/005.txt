The Ration Office noticed the missing salvage log on the third day. On the fourth, two inspectors in white coats arrived at the cooper's yard and asked pleasantly whether Miss Marlowe had been feeling well.
Ines said she had a cold. The older inspector wrote that down. The younger one looked at the stack of prints on her table and did not write anything.
When they had gone Ines burned every photograph in the stove except one, which she folded small and slid into the hem of her coat.
That night she went to find Roe. His door stood open, the lamp was out, and the grey coat was gone from its hook.
