Maro spent the morning in the Council archive, a damp room behind the harbor office where the records of Harrowmere had been left to the mildew.
The ledgers were in good order. Every ship that had ever entered the harbor was listed, with its cargo, its captain and the fee it had paid. The last entry was dated seven years ago.
After that the pages were blank, except for a column of names written in a different hand. There were no dates beside them and no cargo. Only names, one under another, more than a hundred.
He copied the first twelve into his own ledger and told himself it was thoroughness. At the thirteenth he stopped, because it was Aldous, and the line below it read Aldous, Wren.
