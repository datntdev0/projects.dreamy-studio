There were nine windows on the harbor side of the Council hall, and eight of them were boarded over. The ninth had a shutter that no one had been able to close in years.
Lady Sevrin received Maro in the room behind it, a tall woman in a grey dress, with a sunburst pinned at her collar. She did not ask him to sit.
"You have been to the archive," she said. "You have seen the names."
Maro admitted that he had. She nodded, as though he had confirmed a figure she already knew.
"Then you understand why the shutter stays open," said Lady Sevrin. "Someone must be able to see the lamp from here."
