She found him at the valve, an hour before dawn, on his knees with the drill in both hands. A single hole the width of a thumb had been bored beside the seam, and from it came a thread of steam that smelled of salt.
"I am eighty-one years old," said Roe, without turning. "I have waited my whole life to smell that."
"They know," said Ines. "The inspectors were at my door."
"Then we have until noon."
The steam thickened. Under their feet, very slowly, the Glass began to sing.
