The Council met in the hall with the open shutter. Eleven chairs stood around the table, and seven of them were empty.
Lady Sevrin read the names aloud, all one hundred and four, in the order they stood in the archive. When she reached Aldous she paused, and did not look at Wren.
"These are the ones the lamp has counted," she said. "Every night it burns, one of them is allowed to come up the stairs and stand beside the keeper. Most do not stay long."
Maro put down his pen. He realised that the ledger had been the wrong instrument from the beginning. They did not need a count. They needed someone to remember.
