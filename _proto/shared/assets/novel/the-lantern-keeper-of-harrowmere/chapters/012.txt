At dusk Wren stood at the lamp with the bell beside her and the hundred and four names read out. Maro waited at the foot of the stairs, as he had every evening, with his ledger shut.
"You counted the steps," she said when she came down. "You counted the names. What does the sum come to?"
Maro thought about it. "Three hundred and twelve steps, and one lamp. I think it was never about the ships, or the Council, or the debt. It was about keeping the way up open."
Wren smiled for the first time since he had known her. Above them the bell turned in the wind, and rang once, softly, across the water.
